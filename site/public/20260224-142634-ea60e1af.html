Original Review

openai 62.0/100

This is a charming candid pet portrait with strong subject appeal but it’s a bit dim and noticeably soft, especially around the eyes. Gentle exposure/shadow lifting plus targeted sharpening and eye enhancement will improve clarity and engagement while keeping the warm, relaxed mood.

  • Increase overall exposure slightly (+0.3 to +0.6) and lift shadows (+15 to +30) while pulling highlights down slightly (-10 to -20) to keep the white fur from washing out
  • Apply selective sharpening to the dog’s eyes and nose (masking heavily; small radius) and add subtle texture/clarity to fur (+5 to +15), avoiding the background and hands
  • Use local adjustments to brighten the eyes (small brush: +0.2 exposure, +10 shadows) and add subtle catchlight/contrast with a gentle curves tweak on the face
gemini 52.0/100

This captures a lovely emotional moment, but is technically hampered by slow shutter speed resulting in motion blur. The main editing goal is to salvage sharpness on the eyes and snout while brightening the image to make it feel livelier, accepting that perfect sharpness may not be recoverable.

  • Apply AI-based de-blur or shake reduction
  • Increase Exposure (+0.40) and Whites (+20)
  • Warm white balance (+10 Temp, +5 Tint)
anthropic 58.0/100

This is a charming candid moment hampered primarily by focus/motion blur issues. While sharpening can help recover some definition, the soft focus is a fundamental limitation. The image benefits most from targeted sharpening on key features, exposure refinement to prevent blown highlights in the white fur, and subtle enhancements to guide viewer attention to the subject's expressive face.

  • Apply selective sharpening to the dog's eyes and nose area using unsharp mask (amount 60-80, radius 1.0)
  • Reduce highlights by -15 to -20 and pull back whites by -10 to recover detail in the brightest areas of the white fur
  • Increase clarity by +10 to +15 globally to add definition and texture throughout the image
