Original Review

openai 68.0/100

The image has a charming, intimate pet-portrait feel, but it’s slightly warm with a mild green/yellow cast and a touch underexposed. Focus is a bit soft and noise is noticeable in the darker areas; targeted sharpening and noise reduction will improve clarity while keeping the cozy indoor mood.

  • Correct white balance slightly cooler and reduce yellow/green cast (Temp -300 to -700K, Tint +5 to +15); optionally reduce Yellow saturation in HSL by ~-10 to -25
  • Lift exposure and midtones slightly while protecting fur highlights (Exposure +0.2 to +0.4, Shadows +10 to +25, Highlights -10 to -25, Whites +5 to +15)
  • Apply targeted sharpening to eyes/nose (masking or local brush: Texture +10 to +20, Clarity +5 to +10, Sharpening +20 to +40; avoid fur-heavy areas)
anthropic 62.0/100

This is an endearing pet portrait with good composition and a joyful expression. The primary challenges are soft focus and slightly overexposed white fur that loses texture. Targeted sharpening on key features and highlight recovery will significantly improve the image while maintaining its warm, friendly character. The soft focus appears to be from camera settings rather than artistic intent, so selective sharpening is appropriate.

  • Apply targeted sharpening to the eyes and nose using unsharp mask (Amount: 80-100, Radius: 1.0, Threshold: 3)
  • Reduce highlights by -15 to -25, particularly in the white fur areas on the head and chest to recover texture and dimension
  • Increase clarity/texture slider by +10 to +15 selectively on the fur to enhance definition without over-sharpening
gemini 62.0/100

A charming capture with a great expression, primarily hindered by soft focus and indoor lighting discoloration. Editing should focus on color correction to restore the dog's white coat and careful sharpening to compensate for the lack of critical focus.

  • Apply selective sharpening and texture masking to the nose and eyes
