Original Review

openai 78.0/100

The image has strong architectural lines and a compelling, gritty cinematic treatment with pleasing sharpness. The main opportunities are balancing the warm cast and recovering some highlight/shadow detail to better reveal brick texture while keeping the moody, high-contrast intent.

  • Recover highlight detail in the brightest brick areas using Highlights (-25 to -45) and a slight Whites reduction (-5 to -15), while protecting the window glow with a selective mask if needed
  • Lift deep shadows slightly (Shadows +10 to +25) and set black point carefully (Blacks -5 to -15) to maintain contrast; consider a subtle curve to keep midtone punch
  • Cool the overall color temperature slightly (Temp -300 to -700K) and reduce orange saturation in HSL (Orange Sat -5 to -15) while preserving warm sun directionality
anthropic 72.0/100

This image has strong atmospheric qualities with effective use of golden hour light creating drama on weathered architecture. The intentionally moody aesthetic should be preserved while revealing some shadow detail to enhance depth. Minor refinements to tonal balance and selective color grading will elevate the image without compromising its gritty, urban character.

  • Lift shadows in the lower right corner and doorway areas by +15-20 to reveal architectural detail without losing the dramatic mood
  • Apply graduated filter to sky, reducing exposure by -0.3 stops and increasing clarity slightly to enhance the dramatic cloud texture
  • Apply selective color grading: cool the sky tones slightly while preserving the warm golden brick tones to enhance color separation
